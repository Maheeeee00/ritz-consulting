Ask a Question →
What services does Ritz Consulting offer?
Accounting, tax planning and lodgement, bookkeeping, payroll, Xero setup, management consulting, virtual CFO, asset protection and business registration — all tailored to your goals.
How is Ritz different from other accounting firms?
Our advisors have built and run their own businesses. We combine that real-world experience with proactive tax planning and fixed-fee pricing.
Is switching from my current accountant difficult?
Not at all. Once you sign on, we contact your existing accountant, collect your records and handle the entire transition for you.
Can you help during financial restructuring or a crisis?
Yes. We have experience guiding businesses through restructures, cashflow challenges and major changes, with clear action plans at every step.
Which industries do you work with?
Hospitality, construction and trades, retail, healthcare, e-commerce, property, professional services, logistics and tech startups — among many others.
How much do your services cost?
We offer fixed monthly packages based on the size and needs of your business, so you always know exactly what you'll pay. Your first consultation is free.
Do you work with businesses outside Melbourne?
Yes. Thanks to cloud accounting, we support clients right across Australia — VIC, NSW, QLD, WA, SA and beyond.

Ready to get your finances working for you?

Getting started is easy and stress-free. We take care of your tax and financial management so you can focus on what matters most — your business.
